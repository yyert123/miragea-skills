# 3 Questions About Cloned Voices
## Do I need a recording studio?
No. A clean clip of three to sixty seconds, recorded on a phone in a quiet room, is enough to save a voice.

## Can one voice speak other languages?
Yes. The same saved voice can read Spanish, Japanese or Chinese, which makes dubbing a single upload job.

## Where do the captions come from?
The video ships with an SRT file, so you can upload it as subtitles or burn it in with animated-captions.
