# 3 Voice Cloning Myths
## Myth: You need a recording studio to clone your voice
A clean three to sixty second clip recorded on a phone in a quiet room is enough to save a voice.

## Myth: A cloned voice only speaks one language
The same saved voice can read Spanish, Japanese or Chinese, so dubbing is a single upload job.

## Myth: Captions have to be typed by hand
Automatic speech recognition produces timed captions in seconds, and you only proofread them.
