## intro
The derivative of x^2 is 2x. Why? Watch the slope of the curve change as the point moves.

## secant
Pick two points on the curve and draw the line through them. As h goes to 0, the secant line becomes the tangent, and its slope is the derivative.

## result
So for f(x) = x^2, the slope at any point is 2x. At x = 3 the slope is 6.
